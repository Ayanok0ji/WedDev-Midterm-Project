Welcome to Discord. We care about your privacy and want you to understand how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide to us when you create an account, use our services, or communicate with other users.

This may include your username, email address, messages, files, and other information you choose to provide.

How We Use Your Information

We use information to provide, maintain, and improve our services.

We may also use information to communicate with you, protect our services, and provide a better experience.

How We Share Your Information

We may share information when necessary to provide our services or when required by law.

We do not share information except as described in this Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

You can control certain information through your Discord account and privacy settings.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate our services and understand how our services are used.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as necessary to provide our services and for legitimate business purposes.

Security

We use reasonable measures to help protect your information from unauthorized access or use.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact Discord through its available support channels.
